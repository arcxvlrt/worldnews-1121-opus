## Design

- Light newspaper page:
  - Source Serif 4 masthead and headlines
  - Barlow / Barlow Condensed for labels and body
  - a strip of key numbers
  - a region tally bar coloured by region
  - 3 lead and 10 small illustrated cards
  - a world map with numbered pins
  - a 24-line numbered list
  - a date-check box and a label key in the footer
- Every illustration is drawn in `art.py` from Pillow rectangles, ellipses, polygons and lines. Nothing is copied.
- Rendered at 3x and downscaled with Lanczos.
- Checked at full size and at 300 px (`preview-300.png`).
